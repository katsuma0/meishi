Fabric

If you just tapped a card to get here, the thing in your hand is cotton over a firm fused core, hemmed by hand, about 2mm thick. It bends a little and does not crease.

Prices in Canadian dollars, plus HST. No volume discount; each card is sewn by hand and the tenth takes as long as the first. Maximum 25 per order. Two weeks from artwork approval.

What the price includes

The card, the NFC tag, encoding, and setting up the link it points to. Artwork comes from your logo file if you have one. If you do not, I set it from scratch and you approve a proof before I cut any cloth.

If your number changes

The link inside the card can be changed later, at no cost. New phone number, new menu, new Instagram; I re-point the link and every card you have already handed out follows it. Nothing gets resewn.
