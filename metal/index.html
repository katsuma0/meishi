Metal

If you just tapped a card to get here, the thing in your hand is stainless steel, engraved, about 0.8mm thick. It is the heaviest card I make and the one people keep. Highest price, lowest fuss.

What the price includes

The card, the NFC tag, encoding, and setting up the link it points to. Artwork comes from your logo file if you have one. If you do not, I set it from scratch and you approve a proof before anything gets engraved.

If your number changes

The link inside the card can be changed later, at no cost. New phone number, new menu, new Instagram; I re-point the link and every card you have already handed out follows it. Nothing gets reprinted.
