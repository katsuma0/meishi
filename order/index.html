Ordering

Pick a material, send me your logo file, or ask me to set the artwork from scratch. You approve a proof before anything gets made. Then it is two weeks from that approval to cards in your hand.

  1. Pick a material and quantity. All prices are here.
  2. Email or call me. Send your logo if you have one.
  3. Approve the proof I send back.
  4. Orders over $200 take a 50% deposit to start.
  5. Two weeks later, cards. Balance on delivery.

Payment

Interac e-transfer preferred. Card accepted by invoice if you would rather. All prices are in Canadian dollars, plus HST.

Delivery

In person within about two hours of Markham; I am probably driving past you anyway. Canada Post everywhere else.
