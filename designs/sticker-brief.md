# meishi sticker line — design brief

Paste this whole file into Claude (or any design tool) and ask for mockups.
It describes one universal design system; every sticker is a member of the
same family. Do not invent a second style.

## What these are

Tap stickers for shops. Each one carries a microchip with an antenna
laminated inside; hold a phone over it and a link opens (reviews, menu,
instagram, booking, whatever the shop needs). Sold retail one at a time
and wholesale to shops that resell.

## The one rule: universal design

Every sticker, every size, every material comes from the SAME system:

- **Palette, fixed, five inks only.** Indigo `#22304a` (ground), cotton
  `#f2eee4` (paper), selvedge red `#a13d36` (thread, never more than 5%
  of the surface), steel `#878c91`, birch gold `#cbaf82` (rare, for
  easter eggs). No other colours. No gradients except a whisper of the
  same hue.
- **One typeface, never changed.** The same plain sans the meishi.shop
  site uses for "made in markham, ontario" (system sans: Inter/Roboto
  class, medium and semibold weights only). All text lowercase, always.
  No second font, no script, no serif for latin text. The only non-latin
  glyphs allowed are the brand kanji 名刺, drawn in the brush style of
  the site logo.
- **Abstract, not literal.** No clip-art icons (no wifi fans, no stars,
  no map pins). The design language is abstraction of cloth and thread:
  twill diagonals, selvedge edges, warp/weft grids, a single red thread
  running through a field of indigo lines, knots, stitches, seams. A
  viewer should feel "woven / stitched / denim" without any icon.
- **Universal finish: matte.** Matte laminate on every vinyl piece, matte
  uncoated feel on cardstock. Nothing glossy, ever. This is part of the
  brand.
- **Universal footer micro-print.** Every sticker carries, tiny along the
  bottom edge or curved along a round edge: `meishi.shop · made in
  markham, ontario` in the one typeface, lowercase.
- **The functional cue is words, not icons:** a small `hold phone here`
  in the same type, positioned where the chip sits.

## Sizes — most of them small (chip-size)

The majority of the line is small, sized to the chip itself:

- **25 mm round** — the core product. Chip-sized dot. Most designs here.
- **30 mm round** and **32 mm square** — second tier.
- **50 mm round / square** — door and window pieces.
- **89 × 51 mm tag** (business-card proportions) — counter tags.

Design at 4× print size, 300+ dpi equivalents, with a 2 mm safe margin
and 1 mm bleed. On the 25 mm pieces the composition must survive being
tiny: bold field, one gesture, micro-footer legible at arm's length or
dropped entirely on the smallest size (keep it only where it stays
legible).

## Easter eggs (some, not all)

Hidden details for the person who looks closely, one per design at most:

- a tiny 名刺 woven into the line pattern
- one thread in the weave that is red while all others are indigo
- a single stitch out of line
- a serial number, lowercase, along an edge (`no. 0047`)
- on one design only: the birch gold thread

## Materials and quality

- Premium matte vinyl, laminated, waterproof, 3+ year outdoor rating;
  chip laminated between vinyl and adhesive.
- Cardstock versions (400gsm, uncoated matte) for counter tags.
- Cut clean on Cricut; corners and circles must be true.
- These are premium goods; nothing that looks or feels like a freebie.

## Cost and price (context for the mockups, not printed on them)

- Landed cost per small sticker: chip ~$0.60-0.80, matte vinyl and
  laminate ~$0.40, cutting and encoding time; call it ~$1.50-2.00.
- Retail: small stickers 6-8, counter tags 10-12, Canadian dollars,
  bare numbers on the site, singles, no bulk pricing shown retail.
- Wholesale: half of retail, $200 opening order, shipped across Ontario
  and to other provinces by Canada Post.

## Mockups wanted

1. A family sheet: 10-14 designs shown together at true relative sizes,
   so the universal system is obvious at a glance.
2. Each design flat, front-on, on a neutral ground.
3. Three in-situ shots: 25 mm dot on a cafe counter, 50 mm on a glass
   door, tag beside a register.
4. All lowercase, one typeface, matte, the five inks only.
