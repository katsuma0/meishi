Paper

If you just tapped a card to get here, the thing in your hand is 400gsm printed card with an NFC tag laminated inside. It looks like an ordinary nice business card until somebody taps it.

Paper is sold in packs, not singles. This is the stack that sits in a holder by your register so customers can take one home.

What the price includes

The full pack, printed both sides, with every tag encoded and the link set up. Artwork comes from your logo file if you have one. If you do not, I set it from scratch and you approve a proof before anything gets printed.

If your number changes

Every card in the pack points at the same link, and that link can be changed later at no cost. New phone number, new menu, new Instagram; I re-point it once and every card already sitting in customers' junk drawers follows. Nothing gets reprinted.
