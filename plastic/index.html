Plastic

If you just tapped a card to get here, the thing in your hand is printed PVC, the exact size and thickness of a credit card, 0.76mm. It is the only card I make that slides into a wallet slot, and the cheapest custom option.

What the price includes

The card, the NFC tag, encoding, and setting up the link it points to. Artwork comes from your logo file if you have one. If you do not, I set it from scratch and you approve a proof before anything gets printed.

If your number changes

The link inside the card can be changed later, at no cost. New phone number, new menu, new Instagram; I re-point the link and every card you have already handed out follows it. Nothing gets reprinted.
