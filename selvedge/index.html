Selvedge

If you just tapped a card to get here, the thing in your hand is Japanese selvedge denim with the red selvedge line running down one edge and sashiko stitched across the face by hand, over a firm core. About 2.5mm thick.

It will not fit a wallet slot. That is the point. This is the card an owner keeps on the shelf behind the counter and hands to a supplier, not the one that goes out fifty at a time.

What the price includes

The card, the NFC tag, encoding, and setting up the link it points to. Artwork comes from your logo file if you have one. If you do not, I set it from scratch and you approve a proof before I cut any denim.

If your number changes

The link inside the card can be changed later, at no cost. New phone number, new menu, new Instagram; I re-point the link and every card you have already handed out follows it. Nothing gets restitched.
